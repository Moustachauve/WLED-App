### Sound Sync (Beta)
Turn your WLED devices into a music visualizer. Sound Sync captures audio from your microphone and sends real-time frequency data to your devices over the local network using UDP Sound Sync. Dial in manual gain up to 30x, pick from Normal, Vivid, or Lazy Automatic Gain Control presets, fine-tune the Squelch noise gate so your strips stay dark during quiet moments, and configure a custom UDP port if needed. Enable Sound Sync (Beta) in Settings to try it early.
