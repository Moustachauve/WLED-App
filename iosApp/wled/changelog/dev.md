### Sound Sync (Beta)
Turn your WLED devices into a music visualizer. Sound Sync captures audio from your microphone and sends real-time frequency data to your devices over the local network using UDP Sound Sync. Dial in manual gain up to 30x, choose between Normal, Vivid, and Lazy automatic gain control presets, fine-tune the Squelch noise gate, and watch the live frequency bars react to the sound around you. Enable Sound Sync (Beta) in Settings to try it early.
